# Developing the starter

What is needed to work on the starter itself, and not to build a site with it.

## What goes into a library

A component or behaviour of general use goes into svUltra or djultra, so every site gets
it. What is specific to a site stays in the site: its models, endpoints, pages and
components, and any site-specific version of a kit component, as a local copy.

## Creating a site

`setup.md` has the steps, `new_site.md` the values and the host.
