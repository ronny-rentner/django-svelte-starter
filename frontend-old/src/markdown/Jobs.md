# Jobs and Open Roles

**Advance your career with a role at RELONEE Relocation! See our open roles and positions.**

### How the recruiting process at RELONEE looks like:

We want to get to know you and make sure you are the perfect fit for us and we are for you. This is why we want to have a short interview with you and get an impression of your working skills as well.

Case Study. You may be asked to complete a case study challenge designed by us. These tasks reflect real challenges we face in our daily work and provide a glimpse into the role. Focus on delivering quality results, and ensure your submission is clear and well presented.

Tech roles do involve a coding challenge. Through this coding challenge, you’ll have the opportunity to showcase your programming knowledge and problem-solving capabilities specific to the role you’re applying for. Alternatively, successful open source projects can also prove your coding skills.
